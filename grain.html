Storage lists

NGR numbers

Add each National Grower Register number you deliver under. Once there is at least one, every load must have an NGR picked, and Totals can be shown per NGR. Only admins can add or remove these. The share % is the part of each NGR's grain money that is ours; it is only ever shown to admins.

Farms

Give each farm its NGR, then put each paddock on a farm. On the Add screen staff pick the farm (or the paddock) and the NGR fills in for every paddock on that farm.
